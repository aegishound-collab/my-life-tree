why making this web?

Those who mean the world to me and transformed my life.

Each animal shown below represents a person I know; no names are mentioned to respect their privacy.

lion

An ambitious lion is bound to possess a majestic aura and a grand career. He/She is also very likely to become my business partner.

dog

He provides me with an endless stream of confidence and strength.

cat

A gentle cat who healed me. Without him, I might not even be in this world today; he is my most cherished family member.

capybara

He was once a myth, and perhaps he will become the lingering glow of my sunset in the days to come.

As time passes, I seem to have been polished into someone smoother. Despite many unexpected turns, there is no denying that it was these accidents and these animals that assembled me into who I am today.
